How the exchange works

Teach what you know

List a subject you're strong in. Run a 45-minute session with someone a year or two behind you and earn 40 credits.

Spend what you earn

Use credits to book a session in any subject, from a student who's already been through it. One session costs 40 credits.

Keep the guild honest

Both sides rate each session. Teachers with strong ratings appear first, and credits never convert to cash.
